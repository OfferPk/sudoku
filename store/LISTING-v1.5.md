```
Quillnine is a quiet, elegant Sudoku. Every puzzle has exactly one solution and is graded by the techniques it needs, from Easy to Master.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a cell and choose a number
• Each row, column and 3x3 box holds 1 to 9 exactly once
• Pencil in candidates with Notes; they tidy themselves as you solve
• Optional mistake limit, timer and highlighting

COME BACK EVERY DAY
• Today's Puzzle, the same for everyone, plus a 7-day reward calendar
• Rising coin rewards for a 7-day streak (miss a day and it restarts)
• Daily challenge streaks

ACHIEVEMENTS & STATS
• 17 achievements for progress, skill, streaks, daily challenges and collecting
• Stats: games played, best results, coins earned and your longest play streak

SKIN SHOP
• 8 board and number styles (Paper, Midnight, Sage, Dusk, Blossom, Harbor Night, Old Manuscript, Chalkboard) and backgrounds
• Preview before you buy. Everything is cosmetic and bought with coins you earn in the game
• No real-money purchases, no loot boxes, no cash-out

MADE FOR RELAXING
• Plays fully offline
• Friendly first-level tutorial (replay it any time from Settings)
• Sound and haptics toggles, color-blind friendly options where available

ADS
The game is free and shows ads. Optional rewarded ads play only when you tap a button (for example a hint or a free 24-hour skin try). Full-screen ads only appear between finished games or levels, never at launch.
```
