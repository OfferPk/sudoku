## Full description (limit 4000)

**2286 / 4000 characters** (plain text, no emoji)

```
Quillnine is a calm, clean sudoku for quiet minutes. Every puzzle is generated on your phone, checked to have exactly one solution, and graded by the solving techniques it really needs. No clutter, no pressure: just you, a pencil and a 9×9 grid.

HOW TO PLAY
• Fill the grid so every row, column and 3×3 box contains the digits 1 to 9 once.
• Tap a cell, then a number. Switch on Notes to pencil in candidates.
• Use logic, not guessing: every puzzle has one unique solution.

FIVE TRUE DIFFICULTIES
• Easy: hidden singles, perfect for a relaxing warm-up.
• Medium: adds naked singles.
• Hard: locked candidates, naked and hidden pairs.
• Expert: triples and quads, X-Wing, Swordfish, XY-Wing and more.
• Master: the toughest puzzles, needing forcing chains.
A built-in logical solver rates every puzzle, so "Hard" always feels hard, and "Easy" never needs guessing.

DAILY PUZZLE
• A new sudoku every day, created from the date. It works offline and is the same for everyone.
• Keep your daily streak going.

MADE FOR FOCUS
• Pencil notes that clear themselves: placing a digit removes it from the notes in its row, column and box.
• Highlights for the same numbers and for the selected row, column and box.
• Number pad shows how many of each digit are left.
• Undo and erase at any time.
• Choose your style: a 3-mistake limit, free play with auto-check, or no checking at all.
• Timer with pause (you can hide the timer in Settings).
• Auto-save: leave any time and continue right where you stopped.

STATISTICS
• Games won, win rate, best and average times, flawless wins and streaks for every difficulty.

FOUR QUIET THEMES
• Paper and Midnight (dark mode) are free. Sage and Dusk can be unlocked with coins you earn by solving puzzles.
• Soft sounds and light haptics, both optional.

PLAY OFFLINE
No Wi-Fi needed. Enjoy a sudoku on a flight, on the train or before bed.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Quillnine is free and shows ads. Hints and an extra mistake come from optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch.
• No in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by solving puzzles and only unlock cosmetic themes.
• No sign-up and no account.
• Privacy policy: https://offerpk.github.io/sudoku/privacy.html

Sharpen your mind, one calm puzzle at a time. Download Quillnine and start solving.
```

## Claims check

Every claim matches the current game (v1.0.0):

- **Generated on the phone, exactly one solution:** `generate()` / `dig()` in `www/js/logic.js` only remove a clue while `countSolutions()` still returns 1. `npm test` generates 200 puzzles per difficulty and re-checks uniqueness (CI).
- **Graded by techniques; the difficulty list:** `grade()` in `www/js/logic.js` (tiers 1–5 exactly as listed). The test checks each generated puzzle grades to the requested tier, and that the grader never eliminates the true solution.
- **Daily puzzle from the date, same for everyone, offline:** `dailyInfo()` (seed = hash of the local date). Tested for determinism.
- **Notes auto-remove, highlights, remaining counts, undo/erase, mistake limit / auto-check / free play, hideable timer, pause, auto-save:** all in `www/js/game.js` and the headless Chrome test.
- **Statistics per difficulty and daily streak:** Statistics screen.
- **Four themes; Paper and Midnight free; Sage and Dusk with coins:** `www/js/themes.js` (Sage 150, Dusk 300 coins).
- **Hints and extra mistake via optional rewarded ads:** only these two buttons call `Ads.showRewarded`.
- **Coins cosmetic only; no in-app purchases:** correct. There is no billing library in the app.
- **No account or sign-up; works offline:** correct. Ads just don't load without internet.
